## Revenue distinction

Client projects generate income from agreed development work. Sponsorships generate income from agreed placements. Display advertisements may earn revenue only after ad-network approval and eligible traffic. Paying to advertise the studio is a separate expense. The website does not guarantee earnings or ad approval.

## Starting routine

Keep available project links accurate; mark unfinished apps as development previews. Share one concrete project demonstration at a time. Publish useful original guides based on actual work. Review new inquiries in the owner workspace. Track qualified inquiries and completed projects, not just visits. Confirm sponsor fit and label paid placements clearly.
