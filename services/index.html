START WITH WHAT MATTERS

A useful first version.

A good project starts with one clear outcome. Your first version should make that outcome work well.

For an idea taking shape

We can begin with a short feature list, screen designs, or an interactive prototype. This helps you decide what belongs in the first build before committing to a larger scope.

For an established business

Bring the workflow you already use: requests, booking, customer information, or a task repeated every day. We’ll discuss a website, app, or focused tool that fits that work.

For something you already own

Share the current website or app, what works, and what needs improvement. Existing-project work starts with a review of the code and access needed before a commitment to changes.

For AI features or a game

These projects are reviewed individually. The proposal defines the supported experience, required services, ongoing costs, and what can be demonstrated and tested.

PRICING & PAYMENTS

Quoted for your project.

There is no charge to send an inquiry. Project pricing follows a review of the features, complexity, content, integrations, and timeline. Any deposit and milestone payments are included in the proposal before you agree.

Payment options are arranged through verified business payment requests. PayPal, Venmo, Apple Pay, and Cash App are the preferred options; the methods actually available are shown on your invoice or payment request.

Your proposal should make this clear

  • What is included and what is outside the scope
  • Deliverables and review milestones
  • Price, deposit, and payment timing
  • Revisions, changes, and ongoing costs
  • Source files, account ownership, and handoff
  • Support and maintenance arrangements

COMMON QUESTIONS

Good things to ask.

Do I need a finished plan before contacting you?

No. Tell us who will use the project and what they need to do. “Not sure yet” is a valid budget or timeline answer. We can clarify the first useful version together.

Can I see work before the project is finished?

Review points are agreed in the proposal. These may include screen designs, a working preview, and a final acceptance check against the written scope.

Will you publish my idea or client information?

Client inquiries are private. Public portfolio use of client work must be agreed separately. The projects shown in our catalog are Bay Area Apps’ own projects.

Can you guarantee revenue, downloads, or search rankings?

No. We can build and improve the agreed experience. Business results depend on the offer, audience, promotion, competition, and ongoing operation.
