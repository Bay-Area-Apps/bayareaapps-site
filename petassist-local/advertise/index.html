Before you book

Straightforward from the start.

How much does a placement cost?

Request a quote. We will agree on the price, exact location, dates, and payment terms before you pay. Sending an inquiry does not create a booking or a charge.

What results are included?

The package covers an agreed website placement for 30 days. It does not promise a minimum audience, impressions, clicks, leads, or sales. Ask about available, verified audience information before booking.

Will the card look like independent advice?

No. It will be labeled “Sponsored” so visitors can identify the paid placement. Advertising is separate from the website’s service information and care guidance.

Does the placement renew automatically?

No automatic renewal. Any extension requires a new agreement. Cancellation, refunds, and what happens if a placement is unavailable will be agreed in writing before payment.
