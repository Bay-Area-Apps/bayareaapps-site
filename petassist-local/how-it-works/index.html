A practical guide

From visit request to confirmed appointment

Know what happens after you request a home visit, where to ask appointment questions, and how to prepare a multi-pet household.

Request the right visit

The booking page starts with your visit address, then shows the available service choices and prices. Choose the listed option closest to the help you are seeking and describe what you need. The current menu includes Nail Trim, Medication Care, Sample Collection, and Wellness Check. These short menu labels are a starting point for review, not a promise that every procedure or pet is suitable for a home visit.

Ask for clarification before confirmation if you are unsure what an option includes. In particular, do not assume “Wellness Check” means a veterinary examination or that “Sample Collection” includes laboratory testing. For care related to a veterinarian’s plan, explain the request and have the written instructions available so the service can be reviewed appropriately.

Your preferred date and time are a request. Address, availability, the requested task, and practical handling needs must be reviewed before the visit is confirmed. Optional location sharing helps identify your address; it is not live tracking.

Read your visit updates

  1. Request received

    Your details have been sent for review. Save the private visit link. Do not treat this stage as a confirmed arrival time.

  2. Confirmed

    Check the service, scheduled time, and amount shown on your visit page. Resolve any difference from what you expected before paying.

  3. On the way and visit updates

    Use the private page for status updates and appointment messages. “On the way” is a status, not a live location or a guaranteed minute-by-minute arrival estimate.

  4. After the visit

    Keep the same conversation for questions about what was discussed or completed during that appointment. Medical changes or questions about a veterinarian’s treatment plan should go to the treating clinic.

Ask questions in the right place

Use the messages on your private visit page before arrival, during the appointment if helpful, or afterward for visit-related questions. Keeping the details together helps avoid missing an entry instruction or confusing one pet with another.

  • “Which supplies do I need for the service we confirmed?”
  • “My cat hides when someone arrives. What information would help you plan?”
  • “My dog recently came home from the hospital. Should I have the discharge paperwork ready for our agreed visit?”
  • “Can we review which parts of my request are included?”
  • “The gate code has changed. Where should I send it privately?”

For a separate general education question, the Help Line has its own request process. Sending a visit message does not create a Help Line booking. Neither channel should be used to wait for urgent veterinary care.

Plan for more than one pet

Tell us about every pet you want included before the visit is confirmed. Give each pet’s name, requested task, and relevant handling needs separately. A two-pet household does not necessarily need twice the same service: one pet may need a short task while another needs more preparation or may not be suitable for that visit.

Ask for the total price and expected appointment scope for all requested pets. Do not assume additional pets are free or included in a displayed single-service amount. No fixed additional-pet charge is promised by this guide; the agreed service and total should be clear before payment.

Describe hiding, handling sensitivities, previous biting or scratching, and anything that could affect safe access. Plan a quiet space and a way to keep other pets away from the entrance. Read the full home preparation checklist before the appointment.

Payment, changes, and returning to your visit

Payment becomes available after confirmation. Check the displayed amount and use the payment options offered by the checkout. If the amount or payment status looks wrong, ask for help before making another payment.

If your schedule changes, send a message on the visit page as soon as possible. A message requesting a different time is not itself a confirmation of the new time. Ask about cancellation or refund questions directly; this guide does not introduce a new cancellation fee or promise a refund.

Keep your private link in a bookmark or another private location. Share only the public Home Visits address when recommending the service. If you lose access, contact support; do not post the private link in a public comment.

Need a hand?

Use the contact page for help finding the right service or returning to an existing request. Keep private booking links and personal details out of public posts.
