Before we arrive

A little preparation.
A more comfortable visit.

Use this guide to organize your home visit, prepare the information you want to share, and keep appointment questions in one place.

This public page contains general preparation tips. Use your private visit page for your address, pet details, appointment updates, and messages.

Request a home visit

Your visit checklist

  1. Check your appointment details

    A requested date is a preference until your appointment is confirmed. Review the confirmed time, service, and price on your private visit page. Send any changes through the same booking conversation so they stay with the appointment.

    If you have more than one pet, list each pet and what you would like help with before confirmation. Ask how the additional time and total price will be handled. Do not assume another pet is included in the first pet’s appointment.

  2. Make arriving straightforward

    Share the correct address, unit number, parking instructions, and any gate or entry instructions privately. Mention stairs, elevator access, or other practical details that may affect arrival. Arrange for an adult who can provide access and answer questions to be present.

    The website’s “On the way” status is an appointment update. It is not a live map or an exact arrival estimate. If you need a timing update, ask in your visit conversation.

  3. Choose a comfortable space

    Prepare a quiet, well-lit area with room to move safely. Keep the entrance clear and secure exterior doors and gates. If several animals live in your home, plan separate spaces when that would make arrival calmer.

    Keep a familiar towel or blanket nearby if your pet finds it comforting. Ask in advance which supplies the visit requires and which are provided. Avoid buying special equipment until the service and supplies are confirmed.

  4. Tell us what helps your pet

    Share anything useful about your pet’s usual reactions to visitors or handling: where they like to rest, whether they hide, any known handling sensitivities, or a history of biting or scratching. Discuss these details before the visit so we can decide whether the requested service is a suitable fit.

    If your pet’s needs or behavior have changed since booking, send an update. Tell us what you have observed rather than guessing at a cause. You can ask what arrangements would help the visit proceed comfortably.

  5. Gather the information you want to discuss

    Write down your main questions and your pet’s regular routine. If the appointment relates to care already directed by your veterinarian, have their written instructions available. For a pet recently discharged after surgery or an emergency stay, keep the discharge instructions and the clinic’s contact details together.

    A home visit does not replace the instructions or follow-up appointment provided by your veterinarian. Ask the treating clinic about medical instructions you do not understand or changes to the plan. This checklist does not provide treatment or medication directions.

  6. Keep your visit link private

    Save the private link received after requesting a visit. It gives access to appointment information and messages, so share it only with someone you want to have that access. Do not post it on social media or include it in a public review.

    Payment becomes available after the visit is confirmed. Review the service and amount before continuing to the payment provider. Ask about any difference in the quoted total before paying.

Questions that belong with your appointment

You can send questions before arrival or follow up about the visit in the same private conversation. Examples include:

  • “What should I have ready for this service?”
  • “There are two pets at home. Can you confirm the time and total for both?”
  • “My pet is nervous around visitors. What should I tell you before arrival?”
  • “Do you have the parking and entry instructions?”
  • “Where can I find my confirmed appointment or payment status?”

These visit messages stay with your appointment. They do not create a separate Help Line request. For a separate general pet-care question, explore the Paws & Whiskers Help Line and its available options.

Before you close this page

Make sure you know how to return to your booking, what is confirmed, and which questions still need an answer. If your pet needs urgent care, contact a veterinarian or emergency hospital directly; do not wait for a home visit or website reply.
