Bumper Cars Sky Arena

Privacy and support · Updated October 24, 2026 · App version 1.1

What the game keeps on your device

Your three local save profiles keep game setup, completed arenas, wins, garage coins, cosmetic selections, and tutorial progress. Sound, haptics, accessibility, performance settings, and the selected profile are also saved locally. The game does not run a Bay Area Apps account system or upload these saves to our own server. Device backups may include local app data according to your device settings. Removing the app and its data removes its local saves.

Optional Game Center online play

Solo bot play does not require Game Center. When you choose online play, Apple's Game Center handles account sign-in, invitations, matchmaking, and peer connections. During a match, the game exchanges Game Center player identifiers, chosen match settings, steering and boost input, car positions, and match results with connected peers to run the shared game. One participating device hosts the match simulation. The game does not persist these peer identifiers in its local saves or send them to a Bay Area Apps backend. Game Center and Apple's services operate under Apple's privacy policy. Other players may see the identity Apple provides for multiplayer play.

Optional Sky Pass subscriptions

Apple processes purchases, billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds. The game uses StoreKit to check verified subscription transactions and current access on your device. We do not receive or store your payment-card details. Use Restore Purchases in Sky Pass to refresh eligible access. Manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple Account settings. Local coins already earned remain in the garage after subscription access ends.

Advertising and analytics

Production advertising is disabled in version 1.1. The game does not start the advertising or consent SDK in this release and does not add its own analytics, tracking, or advertising identifier collection. Core gameplay and all 12 arenas are available without a subscription.

Device permissions

The game does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library. Optional Game Center and App Store authentication are handled by Apple.

This support page and contacting us

This page is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver the page and maintain security. This page has no advertising or analytics scripts. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to provide support and maintain related correspondence. Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, or verification codes.

Help with the game

Use the tutorial or controls guide to learn steering and boost. Pause offers resume, settings, restart for solo rounds, and a return to the garage. Online matches need the selected number of separate players and Game Center accounts; players are not silently replaced by bots. If matchmaking is unavailable, cancel and choose solo bot play. Leaving or backgrounding an online match ends that connection.

Contact and privacy requests

For support or questions about information you shared with us, email info@bayareaapps.com. Include the app version, device model, and a brief description of the problem. We cannot recover local saves that have been removed from your device.
