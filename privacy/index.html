Website privacy

Updated October 22, 2026

This notice covers BayAreaApps.com and its Paws & Whiskers and Paws & Whiskers Visit companion pages.

Website visits

These websites are hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting and advertising providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser, device information, pages visited, and request logs to deliver pages, maintain security, measure use, and provide advertising.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to support these websites with advertising. Public resource pages may load advertising. The new studio inquiry form, client portal, and owner workspace do not load advertising or analytics scripts. Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies, web beacons, local storage, IP addresses, and device or browser identifiers to deliver, measure, and personalize advertising, including based on visits to this and other websites.

Google advertising cookies can allow Google and its partners to show ads based on your visits to this website and other websites. Other vendors or ad networks may also serve ads and use cookies for advertising and measurement.

Sponsor inquiries and placements

Public information pages may carry clearly labeled sponsored placements. Sponsors do not receive access to private client questions, appointment records, payment information, or contact lists. Selecting a sponsor link takes you to that business’s website, where its privacy policy applies. Studio sponsor inquiries use the project inquiry form. Some older service-specific sponsor links may open your email application. We use the information you submit to discuss advertising and manage an agreed placement. Booking and private visit pages do not load advertising scripts; the separate public visit-preparation guide may show advertising.

Your advertising choices

You can manage personalized Google advertising through My Ad Center. You can learn about choices for participating advertising vendors at AboutAds. Browser settings can restrict or clear cookies. Where an advertising privacy or consent message appears, use its controls to make or update your choices.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and read Google’s privacy policy.

Information you share

The care-line companion page links to its separate request form. Paws & Whiskers Visit includes a visit request form and private visit pages. Email links open your email application. If you contact us, we receive the details you choose to send and use them to respond and maintain related correspondence.

The care-line form and apps

The Paws & Whiskers request form is a separate service. Its care-line privacy notice describes submitted questions, attachments, payments, and service records. This website notice does not replace the privacy notices in individual apps.

Paws & Whiskers Visit payments

Paws & Whiskers Visit uses PayPal to process booking payments, including Apple Pay where available. PayPal handles payment details. Our service, hosted by Cloudflare, stores visit requests, the contact and pet details you provide, your visit address and preferred time, appointment status, messages, service price, and payment or refund status. Our private business app accesses these records through a protected business connection. The secure checkout link contains a random booking reference; anyone you share it with can view its service, price, and payment status. Your private visit page shows your pet name, service, appointment information, your visit address, a location you choose to share, payment status, and messages. It does not show our home, our map location, or how far away we are. Its separate access key is included in the private link, so keep that link safe. Anyone who has it can view the page and send messages. We use your records to coordinate visits, communicate, and maintain related business records; email correspondence remains part of our business correspondence. These private visit pages do not load advertising or analytics scripts. Contact us about your records or privacy requests.

Visit location and distance

Clients do not receive our home address, service-area map point, or distance. Our business app may keep a private service area so we can judge a visit. That point is never sent to the client website or a client visit response. Clients may choose to share a one-time location from their browser while at the visit address; browser permission and confirmation are required. Shared coordinates are stored with the private visit request and can be removed from its private page. We do not continuously track clients or the business phone, and we do not show clients how far away we are. Our business app may send a visit address to Apple’s location service to estimate the client’s location for our own use. The client website does not embed a map of either location. A map link on the private page opens Apple Maps for the client’s own visit address only. Your address and any location you share are private to the business and anyone with your private visit link. Removing client details also removes their stored address and coordinates.

Studio project inquiries and private client workspace

When you request custom development or sponsorship, we collect the name, email, company or website if supplied, project type, budget range, timing, and project description you submit. Cloudflare hosts the studio service and database. We use these details to review the request, prepare proposals, coordinate agreed work, and maintain related business records. Please do not submit passwords, API keys, payment-card details, or sensitive health information in a project brief.

Your private project link contains a random access token. Anyone with that link can view your project, send messages, and approve a proposal. Keep it private, save it securely, and contact info@bayareaapps.com if it is lost or shared accidentally. Submitted messages, proposal versions and approvals, milestones, invoice links, and owner-recorded payment status are kept with the request. The owner workspace uses Cloudflare Access sign-in. Access tokens are not used for advertising. Security logs and short-lived rate-limit records may include a hashed network identifier to limit abuse.

Payment links take you to the chosen payment provider. We do not collect or store card numbers. A payment marked as verified by the business records an owner review; it is not a live balance from a payment provider. Account and device eligibility determine which payment options appear. We retain inquiry and project records for follow-up and business administration; request access, correction, or deletion by emailing info@bayareaapps.com. Records needed for completed work, disputes, or applicable recordkeeping may need to be retained.

Bay Area Apps companion app

The companion app downloads the public project catalog and stores a local catalog cache and your favorites on your device. Favorites are not automatically synced between devices. Private owner access opens in your system browser. The app does not add its own advertising or analytics. External websites and payment providers have their own privacy notices.
