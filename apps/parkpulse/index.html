ParkPulse

A native skatepark discovery app with voluntary activity contributions and moderated park conversations.

Find a park. Understand the context.

Browse parks on a map, search by city, and read park-scoped conversations. Seven official San Francisco facilities and mapped footprints are included offline. Search results outside that set are discovery results, not verified boundaries.

Voluntary contributions are off by default. Published categories describe recent detected app activity when enough eligible participants contribute; they do not measure everyone at the park or establish safety. Community posts require moderator approval.

The app is a native Apple development build. This website introduces the project; it does not collect location or create accounts.

Current status

Private development pilot. Public activation, moderation operations, and physical park field tests remain outstanding.
