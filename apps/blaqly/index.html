Blaqly

An installable business directory with discovery, favorites, suggestions, and administrator-reviewed listing changes.

Discover businesses worth knowing.

Search by business, location and category, open profiles, and follow real business contact links. Signed-in favorites and requests are scoped to the account.

Suggestions, ownership claims, corrections and listing edits enter an administrator review process. Submissions do not publish themselves. Paid listings are not enabled.

The website is also the installable web app. The existing hosted preview remains restricted by its owner settings.
