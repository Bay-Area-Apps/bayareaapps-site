APP PRIVACY NOTICE

Critter Care privacy

Updated October 23, 2026 · Bay Area Apps LLC

This notice describes the current Critter Care native app, including its planned iOS advertising feature. It explains the difference between records kept on your device and information sent to online services. Older builds and the separate Paws & Whiskers service can have different features and notices.

Current availability: Public app distribution is not confirmed. Production ads remain disabled pending a public store listing and Google review. Development builds may use Google test ads. This notice does not announce a public release or an approved advertising account.

Pet records and reminders on your device

Pet profiles, care journal entries, reminder settings, and saved AI conversations are kept in the app’s local storage. They do not automatically sync between devices. Profiles can include a pet’s name, species, breed, age, weight, and other details you enter. Journal entries contain the care information you choose to record.

Reminder notifications require your device’s notification permission and use the records you schedule. You can change notification permissions in system settings. Export care records creates a file containing pet profiles and journal records; AI chat history is stored separately and is not included. Importing replaces the current local pet and journal records after confirmation, with a local recovery copy retained. Files you export and device backups may keep copies separately from the app.

AI questions

When you send an AI question, the app sends your message or observations, the selected pet’s recorded context, and recent conversation context through the Critter Care online service to OpenAI to generate a response. The pet context can include name, species, breed, age, and weight. The saved conversation on your device is separate from this network processing. Avoid entering human medical records, account credentials, or information unrelated to the pet-care question.

Accounts, provider profiles, visits, and messages

Online features use Supabase for authentication and service records. Account registration and sign-in transmit the email address and password you supply to that authentication service. A session is kept in the device keychain so you can remain signed in.

Provider applications and profiles contain the business or professional information entered, such as display name, role, description, service area, offered services, pricing, availability, and optional contact or map details. Published profiles are meant to be visible to people browsing providers. Visit requests and provider messages use the online service and share the contact, request, and visit information you enter with the selected provider. Technical request information is also processed to operate and protect these services. Public availability of an online feature is separate from the presence of its development code.

Maps and external destinations

Clinic search sends the area you enter to Apple Maps. This search does not request your device location. Opening a clinic, map directions, an external website, or an exported file uses the destination’s own services and privacy practices.

Advertising and Google services

Google AdMob provides the advertising service, and Google’s User Messaging Platform provides applicable privacy messages. When the app connects to these services, Google can receive network and device details, including an IP address (which can indicate a general area), app or device identifiers when available, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance signals. These support ad delivery, measurement, service operation, and detection of invalid activity. Google’s consent tools also process information to retrieve, present, and remember privacy selections.

The app requests nonpersonalized ads and disables Google’s publisher personalization and publisher first-party identifier features. Limiting personalization does not mean that Google receives no data. Ad and consent processing remains subject to your selections, device permissions, and Google’s services. We do not request Apple’s advertising tracking permission in this build.

Advertising is confined to the separate Settings & records → About & learning screen. The app does not pass pet records, symptoms, care conversations, account details, or precise location to Google as ad-targeting data. The private pet-care, journal, AI, provider-message, and visit-request screens do not show this banner. This iOS integration does not enable ads in the Mac app.

Your choices

Where required, a Google privacy message appears before the app can request an ad. In Settings & records → Advertising privacy, select Ad privacy choices when that control is available to review or change the choices Google requires for your region. An unavailable or refused ad does not block use of the pet-care features. You can avoid the advertising screen while continuing to use the other app features.

Use the app’s pet, journal, and conversation deletion controls to remove the corresponding local records. Signing out removes the local online session; signing out or deleting the app does not by itself delete online service records or files you exported. Contact the business about online records or account requests. Data sent to Supabase, OpenAI, Apple, or Google is also governed by their services and retention practices; removing a local record does not automatically remove every provider-held copy.

Provider information

Read how Google uses information from partner apps, Google’s privacy policy, and Google’s explanation of mobile-ad data. You can also review Google advertising settings. Those settings do not replace privacy choices shown inside this app.

Other providers: Supabase privacy, OpenAI privacy, and Apple privacy.

Questions and privacy requests

Contact Bay Area Apps LLC at info@bayareaapps.com to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information held by the business. Describe which app and information you mean; do not email passwords, access tokens, banking details, or unrelated private records. We may need to verify that a request concerns your information. Provider-held data is also subject to that provider’s controls and retention practices.

We update this notice when the app’s handling of information changes. The date above identifies this version. The separate Bay Area Apps website privacy notice covers visits to this website.
