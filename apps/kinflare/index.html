Kinflare

A Black-centered community app and website for conversations, circles, private messages, and gatherings.

Conversations, community and gatherings.

Kinflare brings together posts, interest circles, public profiles, private conversations and events. The current release direction is for adults 18 and older.

Native Apple apps and the website share a backend. Livestreaming, marketplace transactions and creator payments are outside the current release. Public signup and recovery email delivery still need configuration.

Kinflare was previously called The Block. Its repository keeps the historical name The-Block-App; its public product name is Kinflare.
