APP PRIVACY NOTICE

Bumper Cars: Sky Arena privacy

Updated October 23, 2026 · Bay Area Apps LLC

This notice covers the current Bumper Cars: Sky Arena native game and its planned iOS rewarded-ad feature. The game’s information website is separate from the playable app.

Current availability: Public downloads are not confirmed. Production ads remain disabled pending a public store listing and Google review. Development builds may use Google test ads. This notice does not announce a public release or an approved advertising account.

Game progress on your device

The game keeps settings and progress in local device storage, including sound, difficulty, chosen paint, completed arenas, wins, best knockouts, Garage coins, and a cosmetic Garage badge. Offline practice uses computer-controlled opponents. This build does not add a developer-run player account or a purchase checkout.

Garage coins are game progress with no cash value. They cannot be transferred or redeemed for money. A completed round can earn coins through ordinary play. The production rewarded-ad offer is configured for 25 Garage coins when the ad service reports that its reward was earned. Development test ads may use a different demonstration reward. You can continue playing without taking that offer.

Optional Game Center play

Game Center provides optional online invitations and matchmaking through Apple. Using those features involves your Game Center identity and gameplay or match information needed by Apple and other participants. Apple handles Game Center sign-in. Offline practice does not require a live multiplayer match. The current online implementation still needs real-account and multiple-device verification.

Advertising and Google services

Google AdMob provides the advertising service, and Google’s User Messaging Platform provides applicable privacy messages. When the app connects to these services, Google can receive network and device details, including an IP address (which can indicate a general area), app or device identifiers when available, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or performance signals. These support ad delivery, measurement, service operation, and detection of invalid activity. Google’s consent tools also process information to retrieve, present, and remember privacy selections.

The app requests nonpersonalized ads and disables Google’s publisher personalization and publisher first-party identifier features. Limiting personalization does not mean that Google receives no data. Ad and consent processing remains subject to your selections, device permissions, and Google’s services. We do not request Apple’s advertising tracking permission in this build.

The iOS game offers an optional rewarded video after an offline round. It does not show banner ads or interrupt a live multiplayer round with this offer. The current Mac Catalyst build does not include this ad integration. The game does not send local scores, selected paint, Garage balances, or Game Center account information as custom ad-targeting data.

Privacy choices and rewarded ads

After an offline round, choosing Load optional ad starts Google’s consent flow to retrieve current privacy requirements and present a message where required, before any ad request. Ordinary offline play does not start these Google services unless you choose that ad option. Open the Garage settings and select Ad privacy choices when that control is available to review or change applicable Google privacy selections. The Garage also links to this notice.

Selecting a rewarded offer is optional. Skipping an offer, declining applicable consent, or having no ad available does not prevent an ordinary round of play. Your device may still exchange technical information with Google’s consent service when it checks which privacy controls apply. Changes to ad privacy choices affect subsequent eligible requests; they do not erase information previously processed by Google.

Keeping and removing information

Local progress stays in the game’s device storage while retained there. Removing app data removes that local copy; device backups can contain separate copies according to your Apple settings. Local removal does not delete your Apple Game Center account or information held by Google. Use Apple’s account controls for Game Center and Google’s privacy controls for information those services hold. Contact the business about information you have sent directly to it, such as a support email.

Provider information

Read how Google uses information from partner apps, Google’s privacy policy, and Google’s explanation of mobile-ad data. You can also review Google advertising settings. Those settings do not replace privacy choices shown inside this app.

For Game Center and device services, read Apple’s privacy information.

Questions and privacy requests

Contact Bay Area Apps LLC at info@bayareaapps.com to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information held by the business. Describe which app and information you mean; do not email passwords, access tokens, banking details, or unrelated private records. We may need to verify that a request concerns your information. Provider-held data is also subject to that provider’s controls and retention practices.

We update this notice when the app’s handling of information changes. The date above identifies this version. The separate Bay Area Apps website privacy notice covers visits to this website.
