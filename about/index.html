What we build

Bay Area Apps LLC is an independent Bay Area app studio. Our current public services focus on two different needs: discussing everyday pet-care concerns and arranging help at home. We connect each customer website to a private app used by the business owner to manage the work.

Customers use the websites in their browser. You do not need the owner’s iPhone or Mac app, an App Store download, or a developer account. The two services have separate customer journeys so an appointment conversation stays with the appointment and a Help Line question stays with the Help Line.

Choose the right starting point

When you want to talk through a pet-care question

Start with Paws & Whiskers Help Line. It offers general education and help organizing concerns for a veterinary conversation. The public page explains the question and phone options, current prices, and Free Community Support. Read the question preparation guide if you are unsure what to include.

When you want someone to visit your home

Start with Paws & Whiskers Home Visits. Enter the visit address, choose a listed service, and send your preferred time and pet details. Your request must be reviewed before it becomes a confirmed appointment. The booking guide explains the stages and payment timing.

When you already have an appointment

Return to the private visit link you received. Send arrival details, questions about the selected service, or changes through that visit conversation. Starting a separate Help Line request is not necessary for a question about the logistics of an existing visit.

Public information and private requests

Public guides help you understand the service before sharing personal information. Addresses, pet details, booking updates, and customer conversations belong in the private service flow. A private visit link provides access to that visit, so treat it like other sensitive account information.

The owner’s app is an operating tool for the business, not an app offered to customers. This distinction matters when sharing the service: send the public website to a friend, not a private appointment link or an owner-device registration file.

What to check before you submit

  1. Choose the service that matches your need

    Read the service description and price. A question request and a home appointment are different purchases. Do not assume one automatically includes the other.

  2. Review the confirmation

    A requested visit time is not guaranteed availability. Keep the confirmation and check the details before paying. For Help Line requests, follow the service’s displayed instructions and retain the return link or confirmation it provides.

  3. Keep a way back to your request

    Save the relevant link privately. If it stops working, use the service contact page rather than creating duplicate requests or paying again.

Support and feedback

For a visit issue, contact the Home Visits service; for an existing Help Line question, use that service’s conversation or contact route. For website problems or general studio inquiries, contact Bay Area Apps.

A useful website report includes the page address without private tokens, what you were trying to do, the message you saw, and whether you used a phone or computer. Do not email passwords, payment-card details, or another customer’s records. Feedback about unclear wording or a hard-to-find button helps us improve the experience.

Need a hand?

Use the contact page for help finding the right service or returning to an existing request. Keep private booking links and personal details out of public posts.
