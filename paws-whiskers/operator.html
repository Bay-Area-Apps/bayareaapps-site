Emergency red flags

Any of these in a message → send the emergency template first, immediately.

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Seizures or collapse
  • Suspected poisoning
  • Hit by car / major trauma
  • Bloat / unproductive retching
  • Can't urinate (male cats!)
  • Pale or blue gums
  • Uncontrolled bleeding
  • Heatstroke symptoms
  • Eye injury
  • Prolonged labor
  • Snake or spider bite

Golden rule: when in doubt, refer out. A referral is never the wrong answer.
