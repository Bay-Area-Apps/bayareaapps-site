Welcome! Here's how it works.

A real person, here to help

You’ll hear from a real person. I’m a veterinary assistant with 20 years of experience in emergency and general practice. I personally read your request and offer clear, practical, general dog-and-cat care guidance in your private conversation or by phone.

  • Identifying general warning signs and helping you choose an appropriate next step
  • General dog and cat care and feeding questions
  • Grooming, enrichment, and basic behavior tips
  • Home care & recovery questions after a vet visit
  • Photos, videos, PDFs, records, labels, and other files for context
  • Preparing for vet or emergency visits & what to ask
  • New and senior dog or cat routine-care basics (vaccine schedules, parasite prevention)

What I can't do: I'm a veterinary assistant, not a licensed veterinarian — no diagnoses, no medication or dosing advice, and no substitute for a vet exam.

How it works

  1. Tap Request support, choose the option that works for you, and share the pet-care topic you want to discuss, with any helpful files.
  2. Send your support request and save your private conversation link. I review paid requests and confirm availability before checkout becomes available on that page. Free Advice skips payment.
  3. Continue on your private conversation page or in an arranged phone conversation, using the support option you selected. If a paid response does not cover the agreed educational topic, you may request a refund review.
