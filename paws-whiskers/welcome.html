Welcome! Here's how it works.

A real person, here to help

You’ll hear from a real person. I’m a veterinary assistant with 20 years of experience in emergency and general practice. I personally read and answer your questions with clear, practical, general dog-and-cat care guidance on your private conversation page or by phone.

  • Identifying general warning signs and helping you choose an appropriate next step
  • General dog and cat care and feeding questions
  • Grooming, enrichment, and basic behavior tips
  • Home care & recovery questions after a vet visit
  • Photos, videos, PDFs, records, labels, and other files for context
  • Preparing for vet or emergency visits & what to ask
  • New and senior dog or cat routine-care basics (vaccine schedules, parasite prevention)

What I can't do: I'm a veterinary assistant, not a licensed veterinarian — no diagnoses, no medication or dosing advice, and no substitute for a vet exam.

How it works

  1. Tap Ask your question, choose the option that works for you, and add any helpful files.
  2. Send your question. Save your private conversation link. Paid options include checkout on that page; Free Community Support skips payment.
  3. Your answer arrives on your private conversation page or by phone using the service you selected. If the response does not cover the agreed question, you may request a refund review.
