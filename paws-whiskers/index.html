    Before you begin

    Who will I be speaking with?

    You’ll speak with me through private messages or an arranged phone call. I’m a veterinary assistant with 20 years of experience in emergency and general practice. I provide general pet-care education and practical guidance within that role.

    What can you help with?

    General dog-and-cat care, feeding, grooming, enrichment, basic behavior, and understanding your veterinarian’s existing instructions. I can help you organise your concerns and questions for a veterinary visit.

    What does Free Advice include?

    Free Advice offers pet-care support for those who can’t afford a paid option. Receive general guidance on everyday dog-and-cat care at no cost, with no explanation required. It follows the same educational scope as the paid support options.

    When do I pay?

    Paid support requests wait for availability approval. If I accept, PayPal or Apple Pay checkout becomes available on your private page. Declined requests are not charged. Free Advice requires no payment or explanation.

    Where do I find my reply?

    You’ll receive a private page immediately after sending. Bookmark it and return to check for replies. This service does not send automatic email or SMS alerts. Phone Support calls are arranged separately.

    What if this might be urgent?

    Do not wait here for urgent care. This service is not continuously monitored and cannot diagnose, prescribe, recommend medication or doses, provide treatment, interpret diagnostic results, or replace a licensed veterinarian. Contact your veterinarian or an emergency hospital. For suspected poisoning, ASPCA Animal Poison Control is available at (888) 426-4435; a consultation fee may apply.
