Prepared October 13, 2026. This is an owner sales and fulfillment guide. It does not record a sale, an active sponsor, or earned revenue.

## Offer ready to quote

- One clearly labeled **Sponsored** card on an agreed public informational page for 30 consecutive days.
- The sponsor's business name, a short approved description, and a link to its website.
- Copy review before publication and one reasonable text or destination-link update during the run.
- The price, page, position, publication date, end date, payment timing, cancellation/refund terms, and outage remedy are agreed in writing before payment.
- No automatic renewal. Further placements require a new agreement.
- No promised audience size, impressions, clicks, leads, sales, exclusivity, or endorsement.
- No placement inside the private owner app, client conversations, intake, appointments, payment, or urgent-help flows. Sponsors receive no access to customer contact information, questions, or records.

Quote one brand/site at a time. If a business wants more than one placement, explicitly list each brand, page, price, and schedule. Do not imply that all three websites are included in a single-site quote.

## Price and audience preparation

Do not use a made-up rate or traffic claim. Before quoting, review any available first-party aggregate reporting and record the exact date range, website/page scope, metric definition, and source. The three websites share one domain, so domain-wide traffic must not be presented as traffic for one pet-service page. If reliable reporting is unavailable, say so and sell the fixed placement without audience guarantees. Do not add tracking just to fill a sales deck without completing the applicable privacy review.

Decide the quote based on the agreed placement, verified reach if available, duration, production work, and sponsor fit. Record the final amount in the written agreement before invoicing. Do not connect an automatic checkout to an unapproved quote.

## Quote checklist

- Identify the sponsor, authorized contact, selected brand, and destination website.
- Review the landing page and claims for a suitable, honest fit; do not imply veterinary endorsement.
- Choose the exact public page and position and check mobile space and accessibility.
- Record the total price and whether any tax applies, the 30-day start/end dates, time zone, and payment due date. Check applicable tax treatment before invoicing.
- Include the agreed cancellation/refund conditions, one-update limit, and remedy for missed display time.
- State that audience, click, lead, and sales totals are not guaranteed.
- Obtain approval of the agreement and final card before charging or publishing.

## Fulfillment checklist

1. Confirm the written agreement, final sponsor link, content rights, and payment requirements.
2. Publish only the approved card during its agreed run. Keep the label **Sponsored** visible.
3. Use `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"` on the paid link. If opening a new tab, identify that in visible link text.
4. Check keyboard access, readable contrast, narrow/mobile layout, the destination link, and the published page.
5. Save the live URL, publication time, end date, and a screenshot in the owner's normal business records. Track the end date so the card is removed on time; there is no automatic scheduler in this implementation.
6. Make the included approved update if requested. Agree on any additional work before charging for it.
7. Remove the card at the agreed end time unless a renewal is explicitly approved. Report only measured results from a reliable source, if any.

## Implementation limits

The inquiry pages make the offer discoverable and provide an email path. They do not create sponsor accounts, accept payments, publish a sponsor card, guarantee email delivery, send outreach, verify ad-network approval, or generate revenue by themselves. Public pages intentionally contain no invented partner names or traffic statistics.
