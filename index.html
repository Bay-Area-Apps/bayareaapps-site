Using Help Line

A real person, a private conversation.

Help Line is general dog-and-cat care education. It is not a diagnosis, a medication dose, or a substitute for a veterinarian.

What you can ask

Everyday questions about feeding, grooming, behavior basics, warning signs, preparing for a veterinary visit, or a new dog or cat. A veterinary assistant with 20 years of experience in emergency and general practice reads the request and replies in a private conversation, or by an arranged phone call.

If this may be an emergency, contact a veterinarian or emergency hospital now. Do not wait for a website reply.

Focused Guidance, $10

One specific topic, answered in private messages. You do not pay until availability is confirmed.

In-Depth Support, $15

A longer private-message conversation when one topic needs more room.

Phone Support, $25

An arranged phone call. Written services stay on your private page, which you should bookmark.

Free Advice

General guidance at no cost if paid support is not affordable. No explanation is required, and no payment is collected.

Read the support preparation guide before you write, then start on the Help Line page. Home visit booking is paused; questions about an existing visit can be emailed to info@bayareaapps.com.
